## What it does

Scores stocks and a portfolio against rules from real market and SEC data, and shows where every number came from.

## Built with

Python, FastAPI, SQLite, yfinance, SEC EDGAR, Ollama (local LLM), pytest.

## How I built it

- **Scoring core:** a deterministic engine with no AI in it; rules live in YAML, weighted by importance and scoped by sector.
- **Data:** yfinance prices and fundamentals, plus SEC filings, insider trades (Form 4) and institutional holdings (13F).
- **AI layer:** a local LLM only explains a score on request, and every call is logged.
- **App:** a FastAPI backend with a stock terminal page and a portfolio page that gives each holding an Action and Conviction.
- **Testing:** 548 automated tests, plus backtests before any new signal goes live.
